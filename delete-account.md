# Delete Your AIM Lift Account

**Last updated:** August 24, 2026

You can delete your AIM Lift account and all associated data at any time, directly from within the App.

## How to Delete Your Account

1. Open **AIM Lift**.
2. Go to **Settings**.
3. Tap **Account**.
4. Tap **Delete Account**.
5. Confirm the deletion (and re-enter your password or re-authenticate, if prompted).

## What Gets Deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes **all** of your data, with nothing retained:

- Your account credentials and sign-in information
- All workouts and workout history
- Templates
- Splits
- Schedule data
- Custom exercises
- Body measurements

This is a full cascade deletion — every piece of data tied to your account is removed from our systems, not just hidden or archived.

## Immediate and Irreversible

Account deletion takes effect **immediately** and **cannot be undone**. Once you confirm, your data cannot be recovered, and there is no grace period or way to restore it. If you'd like to use AIM Lift again afterward, you'll need to create a new account and start fresh.
