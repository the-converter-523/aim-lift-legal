# Delete Your AIM Lift Account

**Last updated:** September 5, 2026

You can delete your AIM Lift account and all associated data at any time, directly from within the App.

## How to Delete Your Account

1. Open **AIM Lift**.
2. Go to **Settings**.
3. Tap **Account**.
4. Tap **Delete Account**.
5. Confirm the deletion.

## The 30-Day Window

Deleting your account signs you out immediately, but there is a 30-day window before anything is actually removed. During that time, nothing is deleted — your data stays exactly as it was, and signing back in undoes the deletion automatically. Once the 30 days pass, deletion is final: every piece of data tied to your account is erased from our systems, not just hidden or archived, and you'd need to create a new account to use AIM Lift again.

## What Gets Deleted

Once the 30-day window passes, deleting your account permanently removes all of your data, with nothing retained:

- Your account credentials and sign-in information
- All workouts and workout history
- Routines
- Splits
- Custom exercises
- Settings and preferences
- ...and any other data associated with your account

This is a full cascade deletion — every piece of data tied to your account is removed from our systems, not just hidden or archived.

## Want It Gone Immediately?

The 30-day window is a safety precaution. If you simply must have all of your data gone from existence before the 30 days pass, do not hesitate to email us at [app-support@aimdevlabs.com](mailto:app-support@aimdevlabs.com). Our admins would be delighted to assist you in this process, including providing every possible assurance that it was completed successfully.
